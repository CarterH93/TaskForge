# Sync Tasks from Calendar

Follow directions on laptop:



Nagivate to your Google Calendar

[http://calendar.google.com/](http://calendar.google.com/)

Select options for calendar you want to add

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-27 at 5.08.51 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click _Settings and Sharing_

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-27 at 5.10.53 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Scroll down to _Integrate calendar_ section

Copy the **Secret Address** link

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-27 at 5.12.20 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Go back to the Task Forge app

In the More tab, navigate to the _Syncing_ section and select _Add Canvas or Calendar Connection._ Paste the link into the _Add .ICS Link_ field.
