# Sync Tasks from Canvas

Follow directions on your phone:



Navigate to your Canvas home page&#x20;

Click on the Hamburger menu icon

<div align="left"><figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/3D13AA17-3732-4926-A956-7090904DBB52_1_201_a.jpeg" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Click the _Calendar_ tab

<div align="left"><figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/0FA8EAA6-DB69-4315-9A3E-E2C632C9DE0F_1_201_a.jpeg" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Scroll down and click _Calendar Feed_

<div align="left"><figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/F067901D-E97B-4CB6-8024-4D0F01E599D9_1_201_a.jpeg" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

Copy the URL Link

\*Make sure to select the entire URL!!

<div align="left"><figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/2034D1E4-EBBA-4ED8-BEBA-B99633D03C52_1_201_a (1).jpeg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

In the More tab, navigate to the _Syncing_ section and select _Add Canvas or Calendar Connection._ Paste the link into the _Add .ICS Link_ field.
